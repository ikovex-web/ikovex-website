SEO

Search Engine Optimization. The classic one: showing up when someone searches on Google or Bing.

GEO

Generative Engine Optimization. Showing up in the answer ChatGPT, Gemini, or Google's AI Overview gives someone, not just in the list of blue links.

AEO

Answer Engine Optimization. Structuring a page so a search engine or AI tool can pull a direct, correct answer straight from it.

Local SEO

Showing up on Google Maps and the local 3 pack when someone nearby searches "near me."

Schema markup

Hidden code on your site that tells search engines and AI exactly what your business is, what it sells, and what it costs.

E-E-A-T

Google's shorthand for Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trust: the signals that make a site look credible.

From the portfolio

Real websites for real local businesses. Visit them live and see how they work for customers.

Leo Optical

Optical store and eye exam clinic in Canada. Website design and development by Ikovex.

Where they started

Leo Optical has been looking after Canadian families' eyes since 1996, but their online presence did not reflect thirty years of trust. The old site was hard to use on a phone, did not explain their services clearly, and gave visitors no simple way to book an eye exam.

What we built

A fast, mobile friendly optical store website with clear pages for eye exams, designer frames and lenses, an easy Book Exam path, and local SEO so nearby customers can find them on Google and in AI answers. The headline line "See Canada clearly" comes to life with a soft blur to sharp focus animation, just like an eye coming into focus. The owners loved that touch the moment they saw it.

The result

Customers now find Leo Optical online, see frames and lenses starting at $199, and book exams straight from their phone. The new website brought more exam bookings and more sales, and the team finally has a site they are proud to send people to.
