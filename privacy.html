1. Who we are

Ikovex is a small IT services company based in Ontario, Canada. We provide day to day IT support, website design and development, Google Ads management and business automation for small businesses in Canada and the United States.

This policy explains what personal information we collect through ikovex.com, why we collect it, how we protect it and the choices you have. We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial and US state privacy laws.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

When you send our consultation form or email us, we collect what you choose to share:

  • Your name and, if you add it, your business name
  • Your email address and, if you add it, your phone number
  • The services you are interested in and the message you write

Information collected only with your consent

If you click Accept on our cookie banner, Google Analytics collects information about how you use the site: pages viewed, time on page, the website that sent you, your device and browser type, and your approximate location (country and city level). IP addresses are anonymized. If you click Decline, none of this is collected.

Technical information

To keep the site working, we may receive basic error reports when something breaks on a page, such as the error message, the page address and the browser type. These reports do not include your form entries. Our hosting provider also keeps standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry, send you a confirmation email and prepare a quote
  • To deliver and support services you hire us for
  • To understand which pages are useful and improve the website, only if you accepted analytics
  • To find and fix technical problems on the site
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising. We will not add you to a mailing list unless you ask us to.

5. Who we share information with

We only share information with trusted service providers who help us run the website and our business, and only as much as they need:

  • Google stores consultation requests in a private Google Sheet, delivers them to our business email (Gmail), sends you a confirmation email and, if you accept cookies, runs Google Analytics
  • Google Public DNS checks that the domain of the email address you enter can receive email; only the part after the @ is checked
  • FormSubmit is a backup service that delivers form messages if our main system is unavailable
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website
  • An error monitoring service may receive technical error reports

Some of these providers store information in the United States or other countries, where it may be subject to the laws of those countries. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

6. How long we keep information

  • Enquiries that do not become a project are deleted within 24 months
  • Client records are kept for as long as we work together and then as long as tax and accounting laws require, generally up to 7 years in Canada
  • Google Analytics data is kept for 14 months

7. How we protect information

The site is served only over secure HTTPS connections. Access to enquiries and client information is limited to the Ikovex team, and our business accounts are protected with strong passwords and two step verification. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work hard to protect your information.

8. Your rights and choices

You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy
  • Correct information that is wrong or incomplete
  • Delete your information, unless we must keep it by law
  • Withdraw your consent, for example to analytics cookies

If you live in a US state with its own privacy law, such as California, you have similar rights to know, correct and delete your information. We do not sell or share personal information as those laws define it.

To make a request, email ikovex.support [at] gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

9. Children

Our website and services are meant for businesses and are not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Other websites

Our site links to other websites, such as client projects and social media. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or the law change. The date at the top always shows the latest version.
